# Privacy Policy

This page informs users of the app **Title Capitalizr** (iOS and macOS) about how the app handles their data.

_Last updated: October 2026 (version 1.7)._

---

## Summary

**Title Capitalizr does not collect, store or transmit any personal information.** Everything the app does happens on your device.

---

## On-device processing

- **Title capitalization** uses a rule-based engine that runs entirely inside the app.
- **Smart Capitalize**, **Suggest alternative titles** and **Review with Apple Intelligence** use Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework (Apple Intelligence) on iOS 26 / macOS 26 and later. Your text is processed by the model on your device and is never sent to Apple, to us or to anyone else. These features are only offered when the device reports that Apple Intelligence is available, and they can be switched off in Settings.
- **Essay formatting** reads the Word, PDF, RTF or text file you choose and formats it on your device. Formatted files are only saved or shared where you send them.
- **Google Docs links**: when you paste a link to a Google Doc, the app downloads that document's Word export directly from Google (docs.google.com). This is the app's only network request. It only works for documents shared as "Anyone with the link"; no Google account is used and nothing is uploaded.
- **Spelling and grammar checks** use the system spell checker (`UITextChecker` on iOS, `NSSpellChecker` on macOS), Apple's NaturalLanguage framework and a small set of built-in grammar rules. They also run entirely on your device.

The app contains **no advertising or analytics SDKs** and has **no accounts, API keys or servers**. Your titles and essays are never uploaded anywhere.

---

## Data stored on your device

The only data the app saves is your preferences (which features are enabled), stored locally with the system's standard user defaults. Purchases of Title Capitalizr Pro are handled by Apple through the App Store; the app only checks with StoreKit whether Pro is active. Text you type is kept in memory while the app is open and is not written to disk by the app. Copying a title to the clipboard happens only when you tap **Copy to clipboard**.

---

## Children's Privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
