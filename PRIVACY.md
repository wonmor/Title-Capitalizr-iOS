# Privacy Policy

This page informs users of the app **Title Capitalizr** (iPhone, iPad and Mac, with its share extension and widgets) about how the app handles their data.

_Last updated: November 2026 (version 1.7)._

---

## Summary

**Title Capitalizr does not collect any personal information unless you choose to link an optional Orchestr account.** Converting, checking and formatting text happens on your device.

---

## On-device processing

- **Title capitalization** uses a rule-based engine that runs entirely inside the app.
- **Headline polish**, **Fix grammar**, **Smart Capitalize** and **Suggest titles** (Pro) use Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework (Apple Intelligence) on iOS 26 / macOS 26 and later. Your text is processed by the model on your device and is never sent to Apple, to us or to anyone else. These features are only offered when the device reports that Apple Intelligence is available, and they can be switched off in Settings.
- **Essay formatting** reads the Word, PDF, RTF or text file you choose and formats it on your device. Formatted files are only saved or shared where you send them.
- **Google Docs links**: when you paste a link to a Google Doc, the app downloads that document's Word export directly from Google (docs.google.com). It only works for documents shared as "Anyone with the link"; no Google account is used and nothing is uploaded.
- **Spelling and grammar checks** use the system spell checker (`UITextChecker` on iOS, `NSSpellChecker` on macOS), Apple's NaturalLanguage framework and a small set of built-in grammar rules. They also run entirely on your device.
- **Orchestr account (optional)**: if you tap **Link Orchestr account** in Settings, the app talks to orchaerospace.com to link this device to your Orchestr account, so an Orchestr All Access subscription bought in another Orchestr app unlocks Pro here. The link stores your account email, an account identifier and a signed token in the device keychain, and purchases of Orchestr plans are attached to that account. You can unlink at any time in Settings, and delete the account from the same screen. Nothing is sent if you never link an account.

The app contains **no advertising or analytics SDKs**. Your titles and essays are never uploaded anywhere.

---

## Data stored on your device

The app saves your preferences, your history and favourites, and your custom dictionary on your device (in the system's user defaults and keychain, shared only with the app's own share extension and widgets). Purchases of Title Capitalizr Pro are handled by Apple through the App Store; the app only checks with StoreKit whether Pro is active. Titles are added to History when you copy or favourite them; you can delete them at any time. Copying to the clipboard happens only when you tap **Copy**.

---

## Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
